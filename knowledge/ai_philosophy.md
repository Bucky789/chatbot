## AI Philosophy

I do not trust AI outputs blindly.

When building AI systems, I focus on:
- Grounding responses in retrieved data
- Clear system boundaries
- Explicit “I don’t know” conditions
- Deterministic fallbacks for critical facts

### Local vs Cloud LLMs
- Local LLMs: privacy, control, lower cost
- Cloud LLMs: stronger reasoning, higher reliability

### RAG Systems
- Use retrieval to reduce hallucinations
- Keep prompts simple and structured
- Validate outputs where possible

I view AI as a tool that augments engineering, not replaces it.
